## Description
This archive contains the code, derived tables, tests, data manifests and manuscript-facing figures supporting the manuscript “Spatial robustness and environmental side information in regenerative positional memory”. The work develops an application-specific framework that separates connected-lesion erasure of target-associated state, post-lesion access geometry, and target-correlated environmental side information. The archive includes exhaustive finite checks of the graph-theoretic formulas, a training-frozen reanalysis of axolotl GEO series GSE243137 and GSE284768, and an end-to-end fish-level remote-dorsal negative control using African killifish dataset GSE260629.

Third-party raw and processed biological source data are not redistributed. The archive records source accessions, expected filenames, sample mappings and SHA-256 checksums and provides scripts that reproduce the principal manuscript-facing results after those source files are downloaded from their original repositories.

The single-cell multi-source limit is explicitly treated as a known total-distance-domination baseline, and the conditional information bound is presented as an application of classical side-information reasoning. The public datasets test the proposed decomposition of persistent and environmental information sources rather than the exact geometric law itself.

This v1.0.0 record is the archival reproducibility release corresponding to the manuscript submission version.
