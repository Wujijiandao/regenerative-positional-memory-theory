# Remote dorsal-fin negative-control sample map

The v0.11 control uses the uncut dorsal fins from fish whose caudal fins were left
uncut or amputated proximally/distally.

The 30K and 50K libraries are technical sequencing-depth versions of the same
biological sample and are not counted as independent fish.

| Condition of caudal fin | Biological replicate | 30K GEO | 50K GEO | Tissue actually profiled |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| uncut | 1 | GSM8121289 | GSM8121290 | uncut dorsal fin |
| proximal amputation | 1 | GSM8121291 | GSM8121292 | uncut dorsal fin |
| distal amputation | 1 | GSM8121293 | GSM8121294 | uncut dorsal fin |
| uncut | 2 | GSM8121295 | GSM8121296 | uncut dorsal fin |
| proximal amputation | 2 | GSM8121297 | GSM8121298 | uncut dorsal fin |
| distal amputation | 2 | GSM8121299 | GSM8121300 | uncut dorsal fin |
| uncut | 3 | GSM8121301 | GSM8121302 | uncut dorsal fin |
| proximal amputation | 3 | GSM8121303 | GSM8121304 | uncut dorsal fin |
| distal amputation | 3 | GSM8121305 | GSM8121306 | uncut dorsal fin |

The uploaded author code independently maps the 30K/50K CellPlex CMO tags as:
- 301/304/307: uncut;
- 302/305/308: proximal;
- 303/306/309: distal.

GEO sample descriptions explicitly state, for example, that GSM8121293 is an
"uncut dorsal fin belonging to fish with distal amputation in caudal fin" and
GSM8121303 is an "uncut dorsal fin belonging to fish with proximal amputation in
caudal fin".
